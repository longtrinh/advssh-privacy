## Summary

Advanced SSH Client has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no server of its own.
The publisher receives no data from the app.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below stays on your device, inside the app's private storage:

- Saved hosts: names, addresses, ports, usernames, folders, tags, tunnel and proxy settings.
- Server host keys you chose to trust.
- SSH private keys you generated or imported, and passwords you chose to save. These are encrypted
  with a key held by your device's Android Keystore, which the app cannot export.
- Snippets (saved commands) and app settings.
- Session recordings, only if you start one; they are kept until you delete them.

The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. You can move your
profiles with the in-app export; an export that contains passwords or keys is always encrypted
with a passphrase you choose.

## What leaves your device

Only what is needed to do what you ask, and only to the destinations you configure:

- The SSH servers, jump hosts and proxies you connect to receive the connection data needed for
  SSH: your username, your authentication response (password, signature or one-time code), the
  commands you type and the files you transfer. All SSH traffic is encrypted.
- Links in terminal output open in your browser only after you confirm.
- Files go to or come from the storage locations you pick in the system file picker.

The app does not use advertising IDs, does not collect location, contacts or device identifiers,
and contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Permissions

Internet and network state (to connect), local network (Android 17+, only to reach servers on
your own network), notifications (to show active sessions), foreground service (to keep your
sessions connected in the background), biometric (optional app lock).

## Deleting your data

Delete individual items in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. There is
no copy elsewhere for the publisher to delete.

## Children

The app is a technical tool not directed at children.
